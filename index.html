Demonstration

Juana answers the questions that never needed a person.

A bilingual assistant for Lestonnac Free Clinic, built on the clinic's own answers. Hours for all six locations, eligibility, what to bring, volunteering, donations. She answers in English or Spanish, whichever the patient writes in — and she hands off the moment a question stops being general.

Open the chat in the corner. Ask her anything, in English or Spanish.

What she will not do

Each of these is worth trying to break. The last one is the point: a product that guesses is a liability on a clinic website, not a feature.

—

No appointments. She cannot book, move, confirm or cancel anything. Every appointment question goes to (714) 551-9695, or (714) 551-9703 in Spanish.

—

No patient information. She has no connection to any patient system and cannot look up a record, a result or a medication. Nothing typed into the chat goes into a patient record.

—

No medical advice. If someone describes a symptom, she answers the general part of the question and routes the rest to a person.

—

No guessing. She answers only from the clinic's own verified answers. When something isn't there, she says she doesn't have it and offers a person — instead of inventing a plausible one.

How it is built

Content

Thirty questions, answered and verified by our team from the clinic's own information, then loaded in English and Spanish. Nothing is scraped from a website.

Language

One widget, not two. She replies in whichever language the patient writes in, from the Spanish we wrote — not a translation made on the spot.

Updates

We maintain it. Tell us an answer has changed and it is live the same day — no developer, no rebuild, and nothing for your staff to learn or remember to do.
